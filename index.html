03

TECHNOVA

TECHNOVA is a Technical Challenge consisting of TechQuiz and DebugX.

The same registered team members must participate in both components.

Teams must follow the instructions and time limits announced by the Organizing Committee.

All questions, tasks, and technical requirements will be provided by the Organizing Committee.

3.1 TECHQUIZ — Official Rules

Total Duration: 1 Hour   |   Total Rounds: 5

Quiz Format

  • Rounds 1–4: Regular Quiz Rounds.
  • Round 5: Rapid Quiz.

Round Names

  1. Round 1 — General Quiz
  2. Round 2 — Technical Quiz
  3. Round 3 — Visual Quiz
  4. Round 4 — Challenge Quiz
  5. Round 5 — Rapid Quiz

Team Arrangement

  1. Participating teams will be randomly shuffled.
  2. Each team will be assigned a team number by the Organizing Committee.
  3. Teams must occupy the position corresponding to their assigned number.
  4. The assigned team number will remain the same throughout the quiz.

Question Procedure & Marking

  1. Every team will receive one question at a time.
  2. The designated team will have 1 minute to answer.
  3. If the team does not answer within 1 minute, the question will be passed to the next team.
  4. The next team will have 30 seconds to answer the passed question.
Answer Situation Marks
Correct answer to the originally assigned question 1 Mark
Correct answer to a passed question 0.5 Mark
Incorrect answer 0 Mark

3.2 DEBUGX — Official Rules

Total Duration: 30 Minutes   |   Total Questions: 6
Questions Difficulty Level
Questions 1–2 Beginner Level
Questions 3–4 Intermediate Level
Questions 5–6 Advanced Level
  1. Each question will contain a programming error or bug that teams must identify and correct.
  2. Teams must submit the corrected code within the allotted competition time.
  3. The detailed marking and submission procedure will be announced by the Organizing Committee.
04

PROJECTX

4.1 Title Approval & Registration

Mandatory Prior Approval

All participating teams must submit their chosen project title to the PROJECTX Organizing Committee for prior approval before starting development.

Title Uniqueness

Title approvals are granted on a first-come, first-served basis to prevent duplicate topics.

4.2 Presentation & Submission Requirements

Slide Limit

Final project presentations must strictly not exceed a maximum of 15 slides. Slides beyond the 15-slide limit will not be considered during judging.

Mandatory Slide Structure

Submissions must follow the required 5-section framework.

Section Section Name Required Content
Section 1 Basic Introduction Overview of the project, core concept, and its primary purpose.
Section 2 Scope of Work Detailed problem statement definition and measurable project objectives.
Section 3 Project Details Screen designs/UI mockups, technical architecture, flow diagrams, and key implementation challenges faced.
Section 4 Outcomes Final project output, working proof-of-concept, and social/industry impact.
Section 5 Conclusion Summary of accomplishments, project limitations, and planned future enhancements.

4.3 Evaluation & Judging Criteria

Evaluation Area Criteria
Technical Execution Technical complexity, architecture design, and working implementation.
Presentation Quality Clarity, adherence to the 15-slide limit, and structure compliance.
Impact & Innovation Originality of the solution and tangible contribution to society.

TECHTALKX — OFFICIAL RULES
