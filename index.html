IMPORTANT RULES

1. Team Composition: Each team must consist of exactly 5 members from the same class. No additional or substitute members are permitted.
2. Participation in All Events: The same 5 registered team members must participate in all three competitions — TECHNOVA, PROJECTX, and TECHTALKX. Team members cannot be changed between competitions.
3. Competition-Specific Rules: The Organizing Committee will announce the rules, procedures, time limits, and requirements for each competition before the respective competition begins. All participants must follow the announced rules.
4. Staff/Judges' Decision: The decision of the staff/judges regarding the competition results is final and must be accepted by all participants.
5. Dress Code: Participants must wear formal attire throughout the event and follow the dress code specified by the Organizing Committee.

1. GENERAL ELIGIBILITY

1.1 The competition is open to eligible students of the Department of Computer and Information Science, Annamalai University.
1.2 Each team must consist of exactly 5 members.
1.3 All participants must carry a valid College/Student ID Card and present it when required.
1.4 Participants must complete the official registration process before the announced registration deadline.
1.5 Participants must report to the venue within the specified reporting time and follow the instructions of the Organizing Committee.

2. REGISTRATION RULES

2.1 Registration must be completed through the official registration form before the announced deadline.
2.2 Each class is permitted to register only one team, consisting of exactly 5 members.
2.3 The registered team will participate as the same team in all three competitions — TECHNOVA, PROJECTX, and TECHTALKX.
2.4 Each team must nominate one Team Leader as the primary contact person for communication with the Organizing Committee.
2.5 Participants must provide complete and accurate information during registration. Any false, incomplete, or misleading information may result in cancellation of registration or disqualification.

3. GENERAL EVENT RULES

3.1 All participants must follow the instructions given by the Organizing Committee, coordinators, staff, and judges.
3.2 Participants must maintain discipline and professional conduct throughout the event.
3.3 Participants must not disturb, obstruct, or interfere with another team's participation.
3.4 Participants are responsible for bringing the required equipment, software, files, and other materials specified for their competitions.
3.5 Any situation not specifically covered by these rules will be handled by the Organizing Committee.

4. TEAM RULES

4.1 Each team must have exactly 5 registered members from the same class.
4.2 The same 5 registered members must participate in all three competitions.
4.3 Team members cannot be changed between competitions.
4.4 All team members are expected to contribute to the competitions and understand the work presented by their team.
4.5 Team members must follow the instructions given by the Team Leader and Organizing Committee during competition activities.

5. PARTICIPANT REQUIREMENTS

Participants are required to bring:

  • College/Student ID Card
  • Mobile Phone
  • Laptop and Charger
  • Internet Connection
  • Required Software
5.1 Participants are responsible for the proper functioning and readiness of their personal devices.
5.2 Participants must ensure that required software is installed before the competition where applicable.
5.3 The Organizing Committee is not responsible for problems caused by a participant's personal device, software, files, or charger.

6. TECHNOVA

TECHNICAL CHALLENGE — TechQuiz + DebugX

6.1 TECHNOVA consists of two components: TechQuiz and DebugX.
6.2 Participants must follow the instructions provided before each component begins.
6.3 The time limit, question format, permitted resources, and scoring method will be announced by the Organizing Committee.
6.4 Participants must complete each task within the specified time.
6.5 The evaluation will be conducted according to the rules announced for TECHNOVA.

7. TECHNOVA — TECHQUIZ

7.1 TechQuiz will test participants' technical and computer-related knowledge.
7.2 Questions may cover programming, databases, networking, operating systems, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, algorithms, web technologies, and other relevant technical subjects.
7.3 Participants must submit their answers within the specified time.
7.4 Participants must not communicate with other teams during restricted quiz rounds.
7.5 Mobile phones, internet access, AI tools, notes, or other resources may be restricted according to the rules announced before the round.

8. TECHNOVA — DEBUGX

8.1 DebugX will test participants' ability to identify and correct programming errors.
8.2 Problems may include syntax, logical, runtime, output, or algorithmic errors.
8.3 Participants must solve the assigned debugging problems within the specified time.
8.4 Solutions will be evaluated based on correctness and the criteria announced for the round.
8.5 Participants may be required to explain their solution or correction to the judges.

9. PROJECTX

IDEA → PROJECT → PRESENTATION

9.1 PROJECTX requires each team to develop and present a technology-based project with a clearly defined problem, proposed solution, and technical implementation.
9.2 Projects may involve areas such as Web Development, Mobile Applications, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Cybersecurity, Automation, IoT, Software Applications, Database Systems, or other relevant technology-based solutions.
9.3 Teams must be able to explain their project's idea, purpose, features, technology stack, implementation, and practical applications.
9.4 Teams must bring all required project files, source code, devices, software, and supporting materials and ensure that the project is ready for demonstration. Teams are advised to maintain a backup of important project files.
9.5 Each team must present and demonstrate its project within the time limit announced by the Organizing Committee. The presentation should clearly cover the IDEA → PROJECT → PRESENTATION flow.
9.6 Judges may ask questions regarding the project's architecture, programming, database, algorithms, security, implementation, limitations, and future improvements.
9.7 Judges may request a live demonstration of specific features or ask any team member to explain the work presented.
9.8 Failure to demonstrate or explain a claimed feature may affect the evaluation according to the announced criteria.
9.9 The project must not contain unlawful, harmful, or inappropriate content and must comply with all requirements announced by the Organizing Committee.
9.10 Exceeding the allotted presentation time or failing to follow the announced PROJECTX requirements may result in a penalty according to the competition rules.

10. TECHTALKX

TECHNICAL COMMUNICATION — Technical Talk + Tech Defence + Q&A

10.1 TECHTALKX evaluates participants' technical knowledge, communication, presentation skills, and ability to respond to technical questions.
10.2 The selected topic must be technically relevant and must comply with the requirements announced by the Organizing Committee.
10.3 The presentation should clearly explain the introduction, technical concepts, working/practical applications, advantages, limitations, and conclusion of the selected topic.
10.4 Participants may use presentation slides or other approved visual aids. All presentation content must be relevant to the selected topic, and appropriate sources or references should be acknowledged where applicable.
10.5 Participants must complete the Technical Talk within the time limit announced by the Organizing Committee.
10.6 After the Technical Talk, the judges may conduct a Tech Defence and Q&A session.
10.7 Judges may ask questions about the presented topic, including technical concepts, working principles, applications, advantages, limitations, practical examples, and future developments.
10.8 Participants must provide clear, relevant, and technically accurate answers. Judges may ask follow-up questions or request further explanation.
10.9 Participants must demonstrate genuine understanding of the topic and should not merely read the presentation material.
10.10 The Technical Talk, Tech Defence, and Q&A will be evaluated according to the criteria announced by the Organizing Committee.
10.11 Participants must maintain professional communication and respectful conduct throughout the presentation and Q&A session.
10.12 Exceeding the allotted time or failing to follow the announced TECHTALKX requirements may result in a penalty according to the competition rules.
